Removed on 25 Oct 2026 (the measured best is now the only path): an old command line that left out `--int8` (the bf16 forward) or `--ts_tau_anneal` (the fixed leak), or, in kernel mode, both `--tail_fp32` and `--lean_tail` (the bf16 float tail with 8-bit Adam, removed 26 Oct), or ran `--grad_accum` > 1 without `--ts_micro` (one update from the summed micro-batch gradients), is refused by `from_legacy`, as are the values marked above.
